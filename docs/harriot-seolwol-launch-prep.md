### HERO LINE
**The moon does not wane here. The eaves move instead.**

*Seolwol — Snow Moon*

---

### SUBHEAD
A full moon, held still over a Korean winter night. And a hanok roofline that passes before it.

---

### THE STORY
Every moon phase watch tells the same story: the moon fills, the moon empties.

Seolwol tells another.

Here the full moon never leaves. It is fixed at the heart of the dial — the moon of the deepest winter, the moon of *雪月*, snow and moonlight in a single word. What moves is not the moon. It is the eave of a hanok: the curve of a tiled roof, cut in silhouette, drifting across the light. The moon is revealed, then sheltered, then revealed again — the way it is seen from a courtyard on a night when snow has fallen and the world has gone quiet.

It is a small inversion. But it changes what you are looking at. You are no longer watching the sky. You are standing beneath a roof, in Korea, in winter, looking up.

### THE OBJECT
A Swiss RONDA 708 movement carries the complication. The moon disc is printed in three passes for depth — a full moon that holds its light rather than performing it. Above it travels the hanok eave, drawn from the roofline of a traditional Korean house, so that the reading of time becomes the reading of a season.

This is Harriot's most considered piece. Not the loudest. The quietest.

### THE CLOSING
Not a complication borrowed from Geneva. A winter night, kept on the wrist.

Seolwol is a living souvenir of a meaningful place — a Korean courtyard under snow — and of the still, bright moon above it.

*Harriot · Seolwol (雪月)*
