## Harriot: Growth Through Content + New Model (3-8 Months)

### Q1 Model Status
- **Launch**: April 2025
- **First Batch**: 500 units
- **Sold**: ~400 units over 1 year (slower than ideal)
- **Final Status**: Keep as limited edition (500 piece run)
- **Insight**: Slow sales prompted strategy shift toward faster-moving iconic model

### NEW: Hanok-Inspired Model (Flagship Launch)
- **Concept**: Design inspired by Korean traditional house architecture (hanok)
- **Design Elements**: Curves, lines, colors derived from hanok structure
- **Goal**: Create evergreen iconic seller that can be produced repeatedly for decades
- **First Batch Size**: 300 units (smaller, faster test vs Q1's 500)
- **Production Status**: Design nearly complete, sampling just started
- **Launch Target**: End of July 2026
- **Post-Launch Strategy**: If sells fast, reproduce; if slow, hold and reassess

### Content Strategy (Now - July)
- **Timing**: Start immediately while design finalizes
- **Content Type**: Behind-the-scenes of design process
  - Design sketches and iterations
  - Hanok architecture inspiration photos
  - Design evolution documentation
- **Format**: Instagram Reels (short, punchy, "I DO" style preferred)
- **Cost**: Free (using existing processes)
- **Goal**: Build anticipation and awareness before July launch
- **Bonus**: YouTuber organically mentioned PAULVICE as "good Korean watch brand"—leverage this credibility for Harriot too

### Offline Store Ambition
- **Demand Signal**: Multiple customer requests for offline store
- **Target Market**: Korean tourists (high volume post-COVID)
- **Challenge**: High upfront capital needed for permanent retail lease
- **Current Status**: Deprioritized until PAULVICE revenue scales
- **Alternative Explored**: Gift shop / tourist retail partnerships (rejected—wrong sales model for 300K won watches; need trained sales staff)
- **Partnership Option**: Expand relationships with existing watch retailers (인투와치, etc.) but margins are tight

### International Growth
- **Q1 Performance**: 50% domestic Korea, 50% US/international
- **Opportunity**: Organic search traffic from US, Google, neighbor searches
- **Next Step**: Leverage international demand with better international customer experience (Channel Talk already set up for English CS)

### Website & Infrastructure
- **Cafe24 Rebuild**: In progress, multilingual (Korean + English) support
- **Customer Service**: Channel Talk (free plan) for international inquiries
